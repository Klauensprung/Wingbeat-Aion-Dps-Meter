is bundled or used as a build dependency. Wingbeat's bounded readers implement
the observed wire fields and reject unsupported layouts. The party latency

Local-player CP field research:
RATmeter 1.11.3.320, BasePlayerInfoProcessor.cs and PlayerInfoProcessor.cs:
https://github.com/Kuroukihime/AIon2-Dps-Meter
The reference searches for adjacent current/peak CP uint64 fields. Wingbeat uses
an independently validated EU record-end offset, with range and ordering checks,
rather than importing that implementation. Other-player spawn records do not
supply CP in this reference; party profiles remain the source for other players.

Additional wire-field research from RATmeter 1.11.3.320:
PlayerStatsProcessor.cs, StatIds.cs, SkillCdProcessor.cs, RemainHpProcessor.cs,
GlobalIdLinkingProcessor.cs and EntityDeathProcessor.cs in the same repository.
Stat ID labels/units and field layouts are used as protocol facts. The new readers
are bounded implementations with EU capture checks; upstream source is not bundled.
The EU stats trailer is retained as opaque, not interpreted as a stat. Short
04 8D death records identify the victim. Longer records with positive killer and
skill IDs also identify the victim and may supply the killer identity. This layout
was checked against preceding hits in EU captures. Empty appearance responses
without killer/skill evidence are not counted as deaths.
